Partnership programme

Earn 10% in credits on every project

Invest in your system today and earn credits for future upgrades. Complete a project, build a credit balance, and apply it to your next one.

  1. STEP 1
    Complete a project
    For example, a RM10,000 System Rescue.
  2. STEP 2
    Earn credits
    RM1,000 added to your balance.
  3. STEP 3
    Apply to the next one
    Automation, or an upgrade.

How partnership credits work

  • 10% credit on every completed project, across all project packages (Rescue, Roadmap, Stabilization, Automation, Custom)
  • Credits are valid for 12 months from the date earned
  • Minimum RM500 redemption, on your next project or an upgrade to a higher retainer tier
  • Stackable: complete more projects, accumulate more credits
  • Earn or use, not both: projects where credits are applied don't earn new credits
  • Tracked automatically; you just focus on building better systems
  • Can't be combined with custom or negotiated pricing, or with phased roadmap discounts

For clients who think long-term: build your system in phases and earn value along the way.

How referral credits work

  • 10% of the first project's value, after your referral completes it
  • Same terms as partnership credits: 12-month validity, RM500 minimum redemption, stackable
  • Earn or use, not both, on any given project
  • No limit on referrals
  • The referred business must be a genuine fit for our services
  • Your referrals get the same quality of delivery you did

Partnership credits policy

  • 10% in credits on every completed and paid project (Rescue, Roadmap, Stabilization, Automation, Custom)
  • Valid for 12 months from the date earned; unused credits expire
  • Minimum RM500 redemption per transaction
  • Redeemable on new projects, retainer upgrades or additional project scope

Phased roadmap pricing policy

  • 10% savings when committing to a roadmap of 3 or more phases up front
  • Minimum commitment: 3 phases totalling at least RM30,000
  • Standard payment terms per phase (50% deposit, 50% on completion)
  • Rates guaranteed for the roadmap's duration, up to 12 months
  • Custom phased plans based on your needs and budget
  • Can't be combined with partnership credits on the same phases
