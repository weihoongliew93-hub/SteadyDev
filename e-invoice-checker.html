Common questions

Is my business exempt if turnover is under RM3 million?
Usually, yes. Businesses with annual turnover under RM3 million are exempt, unless a company that holds shares in the business, its holding company, a related company or a joint venture turns over RM3 million or more. No application is needed.
Which turnover figure counts?
For a business already operating in 2022, the turnover in its financial year 2022 audited accounts or, without audited accounts, its YA2022 tax return. A sole proprietor adds up every business registered in their name.
What happens when turnover reaches RM3 million later?
You must start issuing e-invoices on 1 January in the second year after the year of assessment in which turnover reached RM3 million. Reach it in YA2026 and you start on 1 January 2028. Once you have started, you stay in, even if turnover falls again.
What does the relaxation until 31 December 2027 allow?
Businesses whose e-invoicing started on 1 January or 1 July 2026 may issue one consolidated e-invoice a month for all their sales, within 7 days after the month ends, and won't be prosecuted under section 120 while they do. From 1 January 2028 the full rules apply.
What is the penalty for not issuing e-invoices?
It is an offence under section 120(1)(d) of the Income Tax Act 1967: a fine of RM200 to RM20,000, up to 6 months' imprisonment, or both, for each non-compliance.
