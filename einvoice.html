The reality

The parts of e-invoicing that cause trouble, handled

What LHDN e-invoicing looks like for most businesses, and what the platform does about each one.

Cryptic rejection codes

LHDN's rejections name a rule, not a fix. The common causes are caught before sending; anything LHDN still rejects is shown with its reason and the field at fault. Fix it and resubmit in one click, free.

Silent certificate expiry

When a certificate expires, submissions stop. Upload it once; you're warned 60 days and 7 days before it affects you.

The 7th-of-month panic

March invoices must be submitted by 7 April, 23:59 MYT. You're warned days ahead, and nothing is sent after the cut-off.

Missing buyer TIN above RM 10k

LHDN blocks generic TINs on high-value sales. The platform catches it before submission, so the invoice never bounces. Phase 4 businesses can switch on LHDN's relaxation, which lifts the rule until 31 December 2027.

Validation

Three checks before anything reaches LHDN

Every invoice passes three layers of checks before a submission is attempted. You fix issues in your dashboard; LHDN never sees a bad invoice.

  1. 1 · Structural validation

    Invoice number present? Items valid? Quantities positive? Dates parseable?

    400 Hard reject
  2. 2 · LHDN completeness check

    Seller TIN, MSIC and address? Buyer TIN for sales above RM 10,000? Classification codes?

    Staged for fix
  3. 3 · Product master enrichment

    Tax type, tax rate and classification codes filled in from your product catalogue.

    201 Created

What this means for you

  • Bad data is caught before submission, not after LHDN rejects it
  • Tier 2 soft-rejects land in your dashboard for easy correction
  • Your product catalogue fills in tax codes, with no manual entry per invoice
  • LHDN's own rejection reason kept with every rejected invoice
  • RM 10,000 buyer-TIN rule checked automatically, or relaxed for Phase 4 businesses until 31 December 2027

Good to know

Common questions

How does the platform connect to LHDN?
Through LHDN's official MyInvois API, filing under your own TIN. Today you connect with your own MyInvois credentials, and we guide you through it during onboarding. Filing on your behalf as your intermediary, authorised once in MyInvois, is coming soon.
Do I need my own digital certificate?
Today, yes: you connect with your own MyInvois credentials, which needs a certificate from an LHDN-approved provider. You upload it once in Settings and are alerted 60 days before it expires. Once SteadyDevs can file for you as your intermediary (coming soon), you won't need one.
What happens if LHDN rejects an invoice?
Rejected invoices appear under "LHDN Rejections" with LHDN's reason and the field it objected to, for example "Enter valid e-mail address - BUYER". You correct the data and resubmit with one click. Resubmitting is free: an invoice uses your monthly quota once, however many times it is corrected and resent. Most common causes, such as a missing buyer TIN or an invalid industry (MSIC) code, are caught before the invoice is sent at all.
Can I manage multiple companies under one account?
On the Custom plan, yes: each company has its own TIN, LHDN credentials, digital certificate and submission history under one login. On the other plans, each company is its own account. Talk to us if you run several entities.
Do I have to send every walk-in sale to LHDN?
Not one by one. Record walk-in sales without a buyer TIN; a buyer who wants their own e-invoice can still ask for one. At the end of the month, everything unclaimed goes to LHDN as a single consolidated e-invoice, which LHDN expects within 7 days. Switch it on to run automatically on the 1st, or send it yourself with a preview first; you're reminded from the 3rd if it hasn't gone. A refund of a walk-in sale is filed against the consolidated e-invoice it went into. A single sale above RM 10,000 needs its own e-invoice with the buyer's details, unless your business uses the Phase 4 relaxation.
Does my business have to e-invoice, and by when?
If your annual turnover is RM3 million or more, yes. Businesses with turnover up to RM5 million (Phase 4) are in an interim relaxation period until 31 December 2027: you may issue monthly consolidated e-invoices for all sales, and full rules apply from 1 January 2028. If that's you, switch on "Phase 4 relaxation" under Settings > Business Profile and the platform stops applying the RM 10,000 buyer-TIN rule to invoices dated up to 31 December 2027. Below RM3 million you're exempt, unless a corporate shareholder, holding company or related company turns over RM3 million or more. Exempt businesses may still opt in, which is common when large customers ask for e-invoices. Source: LHDN implementation timeline and our October 2026 summary. Is Premium really unlimited?
Yes, there is no invoice cap and no overage on Premium. Fair use applies: if an account passes 20,000 submissions in a month we'll contact you about a Custom plan, and sustained volume above 40,000 a month needs one.
What happens to my data if I stop using the platform?
You can download all of it at any time, on any plan, including after you stop paying: invoices, line items, buyers, products, settings and logs, as JSON plus a CSV. If you close your account, everything it holds with us is permanently deleted after a 7-day cooling-off period, in line with the PDPA, and we email you when it's done. Your validated e-invoices remain available from LHDN on MyInvois. Keep your export: the Income Tax Act requires business records to be retained for seven years.
Are webhooks and POS connectors included?
They're add-ons, so businesses that don't need them don't pay for them: Webhooks RM 19/month, POS & ERP connectors RM 29/month, Buyer claim portal RM 19/month. Sending invoices by REST API or CSV upload is included in every plan.
My POS system is quite old or custom-built. Can it still connect?
Almost certainly. The adapter system was designed for legacy and custom-built systems. If your POS can export a daily invoice file (CSV, JSON, XML) or make an HTTP POST, it can connect. For .NET systems, SteadyDevs can build a tight integration with your existing codebase.
What is the 30-day trial and what happens after?
All paid plans (Starter through Premium) start with a 30-day free trial, no credit card required, with every feature active. After 30 days you're asked to add a payment method; if you don't, the account switches to the Free tier (10 invoices/month). No invoices are deleted.
Is my LHDN client secret and certificate stored securely?
Yes. All LHDN credentials are encrypted at rest with AES-256-GCM before being written to the database, with the master key stored separately. They're decrypted in memory only when a submission is made and are never exposed in API responses; the dashboard masks the secret.
