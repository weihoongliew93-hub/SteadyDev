Common questions

When is the consolidated e-invoice due?
Within 7 days after the end of the month. For November, by 7 December; for December, by 7 January.
What goes into a consolidated e-invoice?
Sales where the buyer did not ask for an e-invoice, such as walk-in retail sales, added up for the month. The buyer is the general public, with TIN EI00000000010, and every line uses classification code 004.
Can a sale over RM10,000 go into the consolidated e-invoice?
Normally no: a single sale over RM10,000 needs its own e-invoice. Until 31 December 2027, businesses whose e-invoicing started on 1 January or 1 July 2026 may consolidate those too under LHDN's relaxation.
What if a buyer asks for their own e-invoice after I consolidated?
Issue the individual e-invoice and leave that sale out of the consolidated one. If the month is already filed, adjust it with a credit note.
What happens if I miss the deadline?
Not issuing an e-invoice when required is an offence under section 120(1)(d) of the Income Tax Act 1967: a fine of RM200 to RM20,000, up to 6 months' imprisonment, or both, for each non-compliance. Past months can be put right through LHDN's Special Voluntary Disclosure Programme, which runs to 31 December 2027.
