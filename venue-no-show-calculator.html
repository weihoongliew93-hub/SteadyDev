What Venue Booking does about no-shows

Reminder emails

One email before each approved booking, sent at a time you choose, from 1 to 168 hours ahead. Pending, rejected and cancelled bookings are never reminded. Reminders come with the edition sold on our Venue Booking page, not the free plugin on WordPress.org.

Approval on every booking

Every booking arrives as pending. You approve or reject it, and the customer is emailed either way. A pending request holds its slot until you decide, so decide quickly.

Cancelled slots go back on sale

When you cancel or reject a booking, its slot can be booked again straight away. Customers can't cancel an hourly booking online themselves: they contact you, and you cancel it in the dashboard.

Deposits: not yet

Taking a deposit at booking time is on the roadmap, not in the plugin. Today, payment for hourly bookings is handled your own way, outside the plugin.

Reports for your inputs

Utilisation, revenue per available hour and bookings by status, for any period. That gives you the utilisation figure above. No-shows aren't recorded for hourly bookings, so count those yourself.

Common questions

How is the revenue lost to no-shows worked out?
Sellable hours a month are your units times open hours a day times days a week, times 52 and divided by 12. Booked hours are that times your utilisation. No-show hours are booked hours times your no-show rate, and each one is valued at your hourly price.
What counts as a no-show here?
A booking where the customer didn't turn up, didn't pay, and the hour wasn't sold to anyone else. A cancellation early enough for you to resell the slot isn't a no-show. If you take payment upfront, a no-show doesn't cost you that money, but the hour still sits empty.
Where do I find my utilisation?
Divide the hours you sold in a month by the hours you could have sold. Venue Booking's Reports screen shows this for any period, counting approved bookings against the hours your resources are open.
How do I find my no-show rate?
Count it. For four weeks, mark every booking as used, cancelled in advance, or a no-show, then divide the no-shows by all bookings. Split it by peak and off-peak hours if you can, because no-shows at peak times cost the most.
Does Venue Booking take deposits?
Not yet. Taking a deposit at booking time is on the roadmap. Today it sends reminder emails before approved bookings, and you approve every booking before it is confirmed.
